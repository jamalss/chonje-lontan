Our story

Not souvenirs.Memories.

CHONJÉ LONTAN looks toward the ordinary parts of Saint Lucian life that are easy to overlook until they begin to disappear.

The artwork is developed from remembered scenes, objects, routines and places, with cultural specificity treated as essential rather than decorative.
